Limitations

  • Keyword tagging misses indirect wording, and a word can match an unrelated sentence.
  • VADER can misread sarcasm and mixed sentiment.
  • Public reviews are not a random sample of customers, and each source attracts different people.
  • Brands below the minimum sample size get no verdict, so their results are less certain.
